Radio Codes – Quick Use Guide (All Lobbies Included)

Locations:
PH Front = Pillbox Hospital Front Lobby
PH Back = Pillbox Hospital Back Lobby
SH = Sandy Hospital Lobby
10-8 = Available for work / Ready for duty
Use when you are free and ready to take duty. Examples:
10-8, where am I needed?
10-8 available for PH Front / PH Back / SH
10-5 = On duty / Covering a lobby
Use when you are actively working at a location. Examples:
10-5 PH Front
10-5 PH Back
10-5 SH
10-5 PH Front, replacing Aizen
10-5 PH Back, replacing Aizen
10-5 SH, replacing Aizen
10-7 = Off duty / Leaving lobby
Use when leaving duty or requesting replacement.
Examples:
10-7 PH Front, replaced by Aizen
10-7 PH Back, replaced by Aizen
10-7 SH, replaced by Aizen
10-7 PH Front, need replacement in 10 minutes
10-7 PH Back, need replacement in 10 minutes
10-7 SH, need replacement in 10 minutes
If no replacement is made in 30 minutes:
10-7 PH Front no replacement made in 30 minutes, lobby empty
10-7 PH Back no replacement made in 30 minutes, lobby empty
10-7 SH no replacement made in 30 minutes, lobby empty
10-9 = Break
Use when taking a short temporary break. Examples:
Need 10-9 PH Front for 10 minutes
Need 10-9 PH Back for 10 minutes
Need 10-9 SH for 10 minutes
10-9 PH Front by Aizen
10-8 PH Front, back from 10-9
10-17 = Travelling
Use when moving between locations. Format:
10-17 [From] to [To] // [ID]
Examples:
10-17 PH Front to SH // 542
10-17 SH to PH Back // 542
10-20 = Location check
Used when asking where units are posted. Examples:
10-20 PH Front?
10-20 PH Back?
10-20 SH?
Can I get a 10-20 from all active units?
Reply format:
10-4 PH Front
10-4 PH Back
10-4 SH
10-4 = Confirm / Acknowledged
Use to confirm message or location.

Arresting Procedure

*ALWAYS HOLSTER YOUR WEAPON BEFORE DOING ANY ACTIONS!!*

Begin your 25 minute timer to arrest the suspect.

Sir/Mam I am going to be putting you in handcuffs now, please stand still.

Flex your "Z" Muscle to attempt to handcuff the suspect - DO NOT HAVE A WEAPON EQUIPPED
(The suspect must be standing still to attempt to handcuff)
If the suspect does not stand still, warn them and if they continue run around taze the suspect.
Suspect should now be handcuffed.

Inform the suspect you will be grabbing them by their arm now.

Sir/Mam I am going to be grabing you by the arm now.

Flex your "X" muscle to grab the suspect by the arm and begin moving them to the vehicle.

ALWAYS TELL A SUSPECT TO "WATCH THEIR HEAD" AS YOU PLACE OR REMOVE THEM FROM A VEHICLE

Sir/Mam I am going to be placing you in the vehicle now, please watch your head.

When you have the suspect at the vehicle, look at the suspect and flex your C muscle on him.
INFORM THE SUSPECT OF ALL OF THEIR CHARGES AND WHY.
MAKE SURE TO DRIVE CAREFULLY TO DOC, AVOIDING THE HIGHWAYS AND DO NOT BREAK SPEED LIMIT UNLESS YOU HAVE TO (BEING CHASED).

(BADGE NUMBER) To Dispatch, I am 10-17 to DOC with (NUMBER) of 10-15 and (ADDIONALS/CONVOY)

Read the suspect their miranda rights.

You are under Arrest,
You have the right to remain silent,
Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law.
You have the right to an attorney,
If you cannot afford an attorney, one will be appointed to you by the state if available.
Do you understand the rights I just read to you?

If the suspect ignores or say they dont understand it, then you then MUST wait 5 seconds and repeat the rights again (3 in total).
After the third attempt if the suspect is still ignoring you, Inform them that you have read them their rights 3 times.

  • Inform the suspect of their charges at the earliest convenience
  • If the suspect requests a private or state lawyer, pause the 25 minutes timer
  • If the detainee states they have a private lawyer: Detainee must give cell number for private lawyer without delay.
  • If after 15 minutes or after 3 valid attempts to reach private lawyer to be present, inform the detainee of the right to request a state lawyer.
  • ASK THE PRIVATE LAWYER TO SEE THEIR LAWYERS LICENCE

If the suspect wants a state lawyer, radio in to dispatch and ask if any are avaliable.

(BADGE NUMBER) To Dispatch, my 10-15 is requesting a state lawyer at DOC

Wait 2 minutes after department call.
If Valid response to department with "attorney enroute" wait up to 15 minutes for lawyer to show up.
If no Valid response is made, make another department call and wait 2 minutes again, then continue processing.
ASK THE STATE LAWYER FOR HIS STATE I.D

Co-operate with all inquiries from lawyers and request a HC without delay. you have 10 mins upon request to show proof of crime (bodycam footage)

IF A SUSPECT CALLS FOR A LAWYER, MAKE SURE THE SUSPECT HAS NO MASK ON (GIVE THEM 10 SECONDS TO REMOVE IT, IF THEY DON'T THEN FORCEFULLY REMOVE IT), THEN MAKE SURE TO SEARCH FOR IDENTIFICATION. AND SEARCH THEM FOR ANY ILEGAL ITEMS BUT DONT CONFISCATE THEM UNLESS LAWYER APPROVES.

AT THE DEPARTMENTS OF CORRECTIONS (DOC)

Remove the suspect from the vehicle, tell the suspect to "WATCH THEIR HEAD"
Approach the vehicle and flex your G muscle on the car. Select passengers > Click the passenger to be dropped off.
MAKE SURE TO GIVE THE SUSPECT MEDICAL ATTENTION WHEN YOU ARRIVE AT DOC

At the front counter of the DOC ask the suspect what size jumpsuit they are.

Sir/Mam What size jumpsuit are you?

If the suspect does not tell you a size you are free to pick what size you think they need.

/me grabs a *SIZE* jumpsuit from behind the counter

Escort the suspect to a cell, ensure you lock doors behind you at all times.
Inform the suspect you are going to reach into their pockets to find their passport.

Flex Your G muscle, Organisation(2), Find out your personal information(7)

Inform the suspect you are going to search them for any illegal items.

Flex Your G muscle, Organisation(2), Search(2)

  • Any illegal weapons with serial number "XXX" or any goverment agency weapons are to be seized
  • The only weapons they can have will be tagged as "AMMO" and they can keep this if they have a valid gun licence
  • If you confiscate ANYTHING from the suspect you must take a photo and log it in the #confiscation-logs channel on discord

IF THE SUSPECT IS WEARING A MASK YOU MUST GIVE THEM A CHANCE TO REMOVE IT NOW

If the suspect attempts to assault you or escape in anyway, taze and handcuff the suspect then destroy the mask.
Inform the suspect of their charges again and how long their sentance and fine will be and reason for it.
